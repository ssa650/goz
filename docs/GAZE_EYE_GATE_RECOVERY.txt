Implementation
backend/gaze_pipeline.py reads only the validated model folder's single
dataset session. Schema/verdict/held-out/UID/screen metadata must agree;
at least 100 calib rows, 80 probe rows and four targets in each group are
required. Finite 14-feature open-target rows and calibration pose bounds
are checked. Probe support must be >=95%. Missing, mixed, damaged or
insufficient evidence disables alternative recovery with a diagnostic reason.
Reading is bounded to 8 MiB and occurs once before the camera opens.

Generic onset=0.28, reopen=0.18, lid-collapse=0.16 and strict hold=250ms remain.
After >=2s of gating, recovery additionally allows sub-onset scores when
both lids meet the measured calibration floor continuously for >=750ms.
An observation gap >150ms restarts this evidence. Face loss, closure,
invalid eye features, quality rejection, stale frames or non-increasing
capture timestamps reset recovery. A recovered ambiguous sample continues
to need calibrated lid evidence and cadence; losing either gates it again.
Personal blink profiles take precedence and disable this alternate path.
No ambiguous stream samples are used to learn or lower the baseline.

The percentile, minimum counts, wait/hold timers and cadence bound are
conservative engineering choices, not measurements of classification accuracy.
Calibration rows were accepted during gaze-target collection; they support
open-lid consistency but do not supply a validated blink/closure classifier.

backend/gaze_worker.py passes the already validated calibration metadata.
It enforces finite 14-feature model input, finite pose/quality values, the
calibration pose limit (<30 degrees yaw/pitch), calibration setup lighting
(40–235) and face scale (interocular/frame width 0.03–0.40). Gazekit supplies
no prediction-confidence value; none is invented. Finite output is checked
throughout a recovery hold and invalid output resets it. Camera-native
capture times own all holds, coordinate ages and freshness. Frames stale
before or after inference reset recovery. Closure, face loss and bad
observation quality do not invoke the gaze predictor.

Invalid coordinates may be held only for 250ms after a valid point. They then
become a finite center placeholder tagged coordinateState=unavailable and
valid=false; heldCoordinateAgeS is null. No dwell eligibility is assigned to
the placeholder. A new smoother is created after hold expiration, avoiding
old fixation state contaminating reacquired points. Outside-screen model
coordinates remain unclipped, preserving the earlier mapping contract.

Diagnostics remain present in packets and durable snapshots. New fields
include uncertain, recovered, recovery reason/baseline/provenance/count,
continuous evidence duration, validityReason, coordinateState and held age.
Recovered ambiguous samples explicitly report calibrated-open-recovery and
uncertain=true. The existing camera UID and schema3/viewer/display/model
compatibility checks are retained. Calibration/model artifacts are unchanged.

Limits and remaining validation
Recorded results measure eye-gate eligibility only. The historical logs lack
raw 14-feature vectors, brightness, face scale and unsuppressed predictions,
so full post-change valid gaze, prediction quality and accuracy cannot be
reconstructed. Recorded closure evidence is not human-labelled blink truth.
No genuine labelled closed-eye dataset was available. A true partial closure
misreported simultaneously as sub-onset score and calibrated-open lids could
still be accepted; the available evidence cannot estimate that risk.
Synthetic zero false acceptances do not establish a real-world rate.
No live camera/EEG, real-face inference, new gaze accuracy, frontend geometry,
profile display or end-to-end adaptive session validation was performed.
Those remain separate from this source/replay verification.
